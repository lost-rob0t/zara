* Contract analysis
- The r7 lifecycle slice is canonical on the immutable start SHA.
- =TtsOutputBridge._get_engine()= currently latches the first engine-construction exception forever with =_engine_failed=True=.
- A transient provider/bootstrap failure on one turn therefore disables daemon TTS for every later turn in the process, contradicting #29 acceptance that provider failures must not strand later turns.
- Retrying construction for every phrase inside the same failed turn would amplify an outage and log repeatedly.

* Bounded design
- Cache a successfully constructed engine exactly as before.
- If construction fails for turn T, suppress further construction attempts only for T.
- A later turn may attempt one fresh construction and recover without daemon restart.
- Do not change ZARA/1 messages, client playback ownership, provider APIs, cancellation, phrase ordering, or Android code.

* Adversarial notes
- Same-turn phrase storms must not hammer engine construction after failure.
- Recovery must occur only on a later turn; it must not fabricate audio or success for the failed turn.
- A successful recovery becomes the shared cached engine for subsequent turns.
- Exact-head focused/full tests, Nix flake/package, Android and shared-mic gates remain mandatory.
