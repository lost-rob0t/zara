* Prior iteration evidence

- The failure was isolated to =test_transient_engine_bootstrap_failure_recovers_on_next_turn=: the test emitted an =AssistantComplete= containing text without first emitting that text as =AssistantDelta=.
- Canonical =TtsOutputBridge= intentionally synthesizes streamed =AssistantDelta= content and uses =AssistantComplete= only to flush the accumulated remainder/sentinel. Existing daemon tests prove that contract.
- Therefore the r9 production design was not falsified; its recovery fixture was malformed. r10 corrects the fixture to exercise the canonical streamed event sequence and recovers the bounded production change onto current master.

* Selected design
- Move engine-bootstrap failure state from the bridge lifetime to the exact =_TurnAudio= state.
- One failed turn attempts engine construction at most once, even across multiple phrases.
- A later distinct turn may retry engine construction and recover without daemon restart.
- Once engine construction succeeds, the shared engine cache remains unchanged.

* Invariants
- provider bootstrap failure never fabricates audio success;
- same-turn phrase storms do not hammer construction;
- failure state is isolated by turn;
- later turns can recover;
- provider API, ZARA/1 wire shapes, phrase ordering, playback ownership, cancellation, and Android implementation remain unchanged;
- exact-head full Core tests, =nix flake check=, packaged Zara, Android compatibility, both shared-mic lanes, reviews and unresolved threads are required before merge.
