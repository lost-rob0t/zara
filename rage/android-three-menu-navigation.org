** Design

Three primary menus: Chat, Workspace, Settings. Secondary routes are native scrollable tabs. Compact windows keep a left drawer; windows at least 600dp wide expose a left rail too. No bottom bar. Shared semantic tokens and all existing runtime/action callbacks remain in use.

Separate Settings runtime, connection, permissions, appearance, plugins, updates, diagnostics, and about. Consolidate identity/pinning/endpoint/Connect into Connection. Keep gated Projects/Scheduled/Plugins honest.

Use a pure validated navigation value with stable-name persistence and a route-keyed Compose saved-state holder. Back returns through menu roots before delegating app exit. This is not a second task engine, actor runtime, or persistence backend.
