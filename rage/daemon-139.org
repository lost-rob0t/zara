* Contract

RAGE is not a euphemism for "code until CI stops complaining."

Each iteration follows this order:

1. Research deeply enough to falsify assumptions and challenge inherited designs.
2. Architecture/design from the evidence, including rejected alternatives, invariants, threat/failure analysis, acceptance criteria, and the exact gate.
3. Generate/implement the selected design.
4. Evaluate at the exact candidate head with the repository's complete gate.
5. If the gate passes, merge. If the attempt is architecturally invalid or the gate failure disproves the design, preserve the evidence, discard the failed implementation attempt, and begin a new RAGE iteration from research/design.

A stale CI run never authorizes a merge.
