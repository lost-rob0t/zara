* Failure analysis
- Compose and VoiceInteraction surfaces previously walked Throwable causes and rendered raw =message= values.

* Design
- One bounded, cycle-safe =UiOperationFailure= classifier owns UI-facing exception summarization.
- Stable categories are limited to permission_denied, invalid_input, network_error, and operation_failed.
- No Throwable.message content is returned by the classifier.
