** Research
#178 requires clipboard/audio/private data to stay out of logs. Current production Android source has no direct =android.util.Log=, Timber, console println/stacktrace, or native printf/fprintf sinks, but that good state was not enforced by a regression gate.

Existing UI diagnostics are bounded/redacted and remain separate from this platform/console sink contract.

** Design / adversarial review
Recursively scan production Android Kotlin/Java/native source during the ordinary JVM gate and fail if a raw platform/console logging sink is introduced. Keep the rule narrow to direct unreviewed sinks; future reviewed structured diagnostics must go through an explicit bounded/redacted boundary rather than weakening this test.
