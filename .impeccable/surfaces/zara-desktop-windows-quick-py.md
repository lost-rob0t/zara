Scope and mode: Native desktop Quick Copilot and Full Chat; focused operate-and-read surfaces for recurring conversation work.

Audience and job: A keyboard-first Zara user needs to summon the assistant, understand live runtime state, work through one conversation, and expand it without losing context.

Primary task: Compose and submit a meaningful request, read the continuous transcript, stop active work when needed, and carry the exact conversation from Quick Copilot into Full Chat.

Proof and content: Real provider and runtime labels, message roles and lifecycle states, conversation history, and the shared trace itself. Never imply access Zara does not have.

Constraints: Preserve native Qt behavior, readable contrast, Enter and Shift+Enter semantics, Escape-to-hide in Quick Copilot, responsive minimum sizes, and fixed signal colors: mint ready, amber active, red stop or failure.

Chosen direction: Signal Cabin. A charcoal enamel conversation instrument with warm ivory language, restrained illuminated signals, and an open route-divider transcript instead of rounded AI message cards.

Memorable moment: The same titled exchange and runtime trace remain visibly continuous when Quick Copilot opens into Full Chat.

Unresolved decisions: None for this redesign.
