* Mara Mother invariant
Org is canonical human todo/agenda state.

Mara may derive bounded observations such as:
#+begin_src prolog
activity(phone_active).
activity(desktop_active).
activity(wear_active).
activity(commuting).
activity(sleep_window).
#+end_src

Routine schedules are user-owned facts. Medication names, doses, or timing changes
are never invented by a model. The scheduler may remind, snooze inside declared
policy, escalate, acknowledge and record missed/completed state.

#+begin_example

Missing, stale, skipped, cancelled, pending or old-SHA evidence is RED.

* Knowledge invariant
Remote/search/model material first becomes untrusted evidence facts with provenance.
Promotion to trusted executable KB/rules requires contradiction checks, tests and
Prolog verification. Search snippets never become executable source merely because
they parse as text.
